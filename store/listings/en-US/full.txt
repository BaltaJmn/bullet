One-time purchase, no account, no ads. Bobbin is a digital bullet journal that keeps the paper method as it is: rapid logging, three bullets, five task states and migration by hand, one entry at a time.

It opens on Today with the keyboard up. Write a line, press Enter, write the next one. Nothing to set up first, no templates, no stickers: a bujo app and daily planner that looks like your notebook.

RAPID LOGGING, THE REAL ONE
A dot for a task, a circle for an event, a dash for a note. Type o, - or a signifier at the start of a line and the bullet changes as you write. A task is open, done, migrated, scheduled or discarded, and priority, inspiration and explore mark what matters. The Key explains every symbol.

DAILY LOG, MONTHLY LOG, FUTURE LOG AND INDEX
Today is the Daily Log. Month is the Monthly Log: the calendar as a list of days, and the tasks of the month. Future is the Future Log, in blocks of months. The Index lists every month you wrote in and every collection, in the order you started them.

MIGRATION BY HAND
Nothing moves by itself. Yesterday's open tasks do not show up today, a new month does not copy the last one, and the Future Log waits for you. When a month ends, Review shows one open task at a time: migrate it, schedule it, move it to a collection or discard it. A task migrated three times says so, and that is the only pressure Bobbin applies.

READ AGAIN BEFORE DECIDING
Every review starts by reading the period again, with room for a short reflection. An optional daily reminder, off by default, with a fixed text that never quotes your journal.

COLLECTIONS AND TRACKERS
Collections for books, trips or anything with a name. A tracker takes one tap per day for the rows you define, one page per month. Bobbin keeps no habit statistics and no streaks: for that there is Quilt, a sister app, a habit tracker with reminders and statistics.

YOUR JOURNAL STAYS ON YOUR PHONE
No account, no cloud of ours, no analytics. Export the whole journal as a zip with readable Markdown, import it on another phone without losing anything, and lock it with your fingerprint, your face or your screen lock. Share a day, a month or a collection as a dotted page.

WIDGET AND TABLET
A Today widget with open tasks, done tasks and events, never your text. On a tablet, the Index and the Future Log open as a two page spread, like a digital planner lying open.

FREE, FOR GOOD
The whole method, search, export and import, sharing, the reminder, the lock and the Today widget: free, with no time limit.

BOBBIN PRO, ONE PAYMENT
Seven more covers, lined, grid and blank paper, more than one tracker and the month widget. Paid once, never a subscription.

WHAT YOU WILL NOT FIND
No AI summing up your days, no mental health promises, no streaks, no ads, no analytics. A journal app for writing and deciding, not a productivity journal that grades you. If what you want is one line a day to read again every year, try Purl.
